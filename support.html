BTB Workout Log

BTB Workout Log is a simple, offline-first workout tracking app for strength training. This page provides support information and contact details.

Contact Us

btb.workout.log@gmail.com

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can use the app in guest mode without creating an account. Your data is stored locally on your device.

How do I sync data across multiple devices?

Create an account from the Settings screen. Once signed in, your workout data will be synced automatically across devices using the same account.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account. All associated data will be permanently removed from our servers.

How do I export my workout data?

Use the share feature on the workout detail screen to export workout summaries as text.

Reporting Bugs

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Your device model and OS version
  • The app version (shown in Settings)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots if possible
